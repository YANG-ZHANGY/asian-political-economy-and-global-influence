# Institutions and decision rights in Taiwan and China

[繁體中文](institutions-and-decision-rights.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Research overview

## Research question

How do institutions distribute authority to make, contest and revise collective decisions?

## The idea

The Taiwan strand examines Indigenous recognition, collective jurisdiction and the governance of land and environmental risk. The China strand considers strategic upgrading, competitive neutrality and entrepreneurial dynamism. Their shared interest is how institutional arrangements organise knowledge, resources and the capacity to correct decisions.

## Connections
