# Model attraction and international leadership

[繁體中文](models-and-leadership.zh-Hant.md) · [Theme overview](../README.md)

Research overview

## Research question

Can people find a foreign institutional model attractive while preferring another country to lead internationally?

## The idea

This research separates the appeal of domestic institutions from preferences about international leadership. It asks how public diplomacy and state-media exposure change combinations of political and economic model preferences, connecting policy learning with the international contest for influence.

## Connections

This project belongs to [Asian Political Economy and Global Influence](../README.md). It forms part of my broader research on comparative politics and international political economy.
